Stop at **GOD’S SPRING BEST CARE HOSPITAL**, just before Rubber Research Junction, opposite **SOLID FFLO ASPHALT COMPANY LTD, KM 11**, and cross the road. Take the street beside the company, turn left, then take the second street on the right. The school is **behind MEA Filling Station**.
